Why it matters: the mismatch can make a new buyer unsure whether FlowTask is a company-memory product or an outbound-sales platform. It also weakens crawler context and makes the strongest proof on the page support a different buying decision.

Bounded repair

  • Inventory every public claim, quote, schema field and article card.
  • Define one canonical Company Brain positioning schema.
  • Keep only attributable proof that supports the current product.
  • Correct legal and support routes without changing policy meaning.
  • Add a pre-publish content-integrity test.

Acceptance checks

  • Hero, JSON-LD, Open Graph and page copy share one category.
  • No outbound-sales legacy terms remain outside an approved history page.
  • Every testimonial has an owner, source and current-use tag.
  • Every public mailto uses the canonical support address.
  • A regression check blocks stale terms from returning.
