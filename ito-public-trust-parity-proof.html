Recommended public wording

Precise without weakening the value proposition

Isolated, encrypted test execution with no intentional source-code retention after active runs. Review artifacts such as videos, screenshots, and logs are encrypted and retained under the published artifact policy. PR diffs and limited context are processed by approved model providers under contractual controls.

Compliance status should be a separate live field: either "SOC 2 Type II audit in progress" or "SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA," depending on the current evidence.
