"Local-first" needs one explicit data map.

BePurple's homepage, privacy policy and Terms each describe a different part of the same boundary. None is necessarily wrong; together they make it hard for a security-minded buyer to answer what stays local, what syncs to BePurple and what reaches a model provider.
