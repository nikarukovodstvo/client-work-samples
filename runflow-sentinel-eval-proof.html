Decision invariants

Every criterion has an owner, threshold, failure action, and measurable regression contract.

Rubrics follow the use caseIdentity dominates headshots; text and geometry dominate product cards; temporal consistency dominates video.
Uncertain is not the same as badA review band prevents confident automation from hiding calibration gaps or rare edge cases.
Hard gates stay absoluteMissing subject, unsafe content, severe identity drift, or corrupt frames cannot be averaged away.
Every model swap proves itselfVersioned fixtures compare false-pass, false-reject, latency, and cost before promotion.
