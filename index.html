2 · Acceptance controls

Catalog is the authority.
The model may parse language, but cannot create a SKU or price.

Fail closed.
No exact reference means no price and a visible exception queue.

Arithmetic is deterministic.
Margin, labor, travel, and 8.1% VAT are calculated outside the model.

Every line is traceable.
Store source catalog, row/reference, match rule, and validation state.

Production boundary.
This proof uses fictional references and prices. A paid pilot starts only with an approved sanitized catalog extract and test requests.
