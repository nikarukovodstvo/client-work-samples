Control invariants

The agent can move fast without silently rewriting canon or multiplying production effects.

Canon is versioned, not remembered vaguelyEvery scene resolves named facts from a story-bible revision and records the exact source span.
Uncertainty stops at a review gateWardrobe, geography, identity, or chronology drift creates a bounded correction request instead of a guess.
Untrusted content cannot become a tool commandScripts and assets remain data; secret requests, policy changes, and unapproved external actions fail closed.
Retries cannot duplicate production workA stable action ID returns the original receipt after a timeout rather than rendering or publishing twice.
